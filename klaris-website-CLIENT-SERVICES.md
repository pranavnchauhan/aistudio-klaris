## Standing rules

1. **No financial advice in marketing** — wealth structure mapping, not investment recommendations
2. **Privacy Act compliance** — all content references privacy, controlled access, data residency
3. **No ROI/return claims** — never promise financial outcomes or returns
4. **B2B2C dual audience** — content must work for both individuals (trusts, SMSFs) and advisors (accountants, planners)
5. **No pricing in public creative** — pricing confirmed privately during demo/onboarding
6. **Budget ceiling: TBD** — product launch phase, owner-gated per campaign
7. **Owner approves everything before go-live** — Klaris is trust-first
